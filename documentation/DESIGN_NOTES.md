# Urban Atlas — Edition 02

An architectural exhibition theme in forest ink, warm paper, chartreuse and burnt orange.

- Oversized condensed titles, archival labels and fine registration marks.
- Layered master-plan sheets on the cover, with gentle pointer-responsive movement.
- Four distinct chapter openings with abstract principle glyphs and full source photographs.
- Alternating paper and dark evidence panels, full images and a ten-policy chapter index.
- A score ticket beside the complete interactive evaluation matrix.
- A closing composition joining the master plan, conclusions and chapter summaries.
- Directional scene changes, staggered chapter shutters, evidence-panel entrances and counting scores.
- Reduced-motion control and operating-system preference support.

The original academic data and evidence pairings are retained. Decorative glyphs are abstract, not site geometry. Photographs remain uncropped; separate annotations are aligned within their original image bounds.

All forty scenes were generated and checked programmatically. Browser rendering and interaction testing remain unverified in this environment. See QA_REPORT.md for the exact limits and launch check.
