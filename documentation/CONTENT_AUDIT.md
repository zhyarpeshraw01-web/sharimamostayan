## Authority and preservation

Original research remains available in the unchanged PDF and in the source-note overlay for every scene.

The four-principle matrix on physical page 38 governs all displayed policy scores. Administrative statements are source-reported findings; the images alone do not verify regulations, grants, review boards or programmes. A zero may reflect non-applicability (TRANS 10) or no identified reuse need (MLU 07), as explained in the source.

## Image provenance

IMAGE_MAPPING.csv contains 62 source evidence-image placements, including maps and the TRANS 03 plan detail. Original bytes were extracted directly from PDF image objects and compared back to those objects. Every row records the physical page, image number, PDF object reference, placement bounds, original caption, policy ID, intended scene and SHA-256 digest.

The extracted images are uncropped and unretouched. The app sizes each image to contain its full original bounds. Annotation overlays are separate from the original files. All photographs and plans open in the enlargement viewer.

Additional intentional uses: P01-E01 returns on scene 40; P14-E02 can be opened from the Rasan Towers observation on scene 5. Policy evidence is reused unchanged in the matrix detail panels and evidence viewer. The overview renders the actual scene compositions at thumbnail size.

Decorative PDF separators, score-cell raster swatches and percentage strips were not extracted as photographs; they were rebuilt with CSS and SVG. The final app does not use full-page PDF screenshots as its scenes.
